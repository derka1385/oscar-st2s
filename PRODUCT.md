# Oscar
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User preference: Next.js, TypeScript, React Three Fiber, Drei, Three.js, Zustand, Framer Motion, Tailwind. Sites uses the compatible Vinext runtime.
## Users
French ST2S students revising BPH skeleton organisation on laptops and tablets.
## Product Purpose
Students manipulate the anatomy lesson itself, explore named structures and answer exercises directly on a 3D skeleton.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Full supplied skeleton hierarchy; exploration, isolation, layers, labels, camera presets, five exercise types, paper-style worksheet, mastery, spaced repetition and localStorage. No supplied GLB; a procedural placeholder is explicitly authorized. Replaceable model adapter must separate mesh names from educational IDs. Additional anatomy modules follow later.
## Brand Commitments
Quiet premium medical education; warm off-white, ivory bones, Inter, neutral panels and restrained semantic group colors. French student-facing copy. References: Apple, Linear, Arc, AnatoPass.
## Evidence on Hand
The detailed user-provided ST2S brief. No source worksheet image and no anatomical GLB supplied. Model is a simplified learning reference, not a realistic anatomical asset.
## Product Principles
The model is the primary learning surface. Progress starts honestly at zero. Labels and group numbers complement colors. Content and assessment logic remain outside visual components.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard structure selection and alternate assessment controls; group labels and numbers; reduced motion support; tablet collapsible panels and touch camera controls.
