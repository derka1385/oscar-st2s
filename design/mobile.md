# Mobile revision workspace

Mode: Operate. Scope: viewport widths up to 767px only. Preserve the PC interface.

Primary task: explore the skeleton, select a bone and learn it without competing panels. The user reports that everything on the phone feels too crowded.

Use a model-first screen with labeled bottom navigation. Move secondary controls and bone details into native dialogs. Show labels only on request. Search provides an accessible alternative to precise 3D selection. Keep quiz questions in a separately scrollable panel and worksheet answers in a readable numbered list. Do not expose a bone-answer list for family or ordering exercises.

Success: search/select/open/close a bone; change the view; answer a quiz; fill and correct a worksheet; inspect progress; no horizontal scroll. Touch targets at least 44px, form text at least 16px, respect safe areas and dynamic viewport. Confirm desktop retains its index, rail, stage controls and detail panel.
